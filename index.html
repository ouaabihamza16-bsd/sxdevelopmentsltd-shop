About the company

An owner-run property company in Slough

SX DEVELOPMENTS LTD is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 15854710. It was incorporated on 23 July 2024 and is based in Slough, Berkshire.

The company buys, sells and lets its own property. Its registered activities are the buying and selling of own real estate and the letting and operating of own or leased real estate.

Not an estate or letting agent

SX Developments Ltd deals only in property it owns or leases itself. It does not act as an agent for other landlords, sellers or buyers, and it does not market, sell, let or manage property on anyone else’s behalf.

What we do

Four parts of one property business

Everything below relates to property the company owns or leases itself.

Property acquisition

The company buys property to hold in its own portfolio. If you own a property and would like to talk about selling it directly to the company, you are welcome to write to us.

Property sales

From time to time the company sells properties from its own portfolio. As the owner, it deals with buyers and their advisers itself.

Lettings (own portfolio)

Homes the company owns or leases are let directly to tenants. There is no agent in the middle: the tenancy is with SX Developments Ltd itself.

Property management (own portfolio)

The company looks after the upkeep and day-to-day running of the properties it owns or leases. It does not offer management services for property belonging to others.

Why deal with us

Direct, local and on the public record

  • You deal with the owner
